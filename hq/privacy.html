Privacy Policy

Adzesua HQ · Effective 24 November 2026

Who we are

Adzesua Connect Limited (“Adzesua Connect”, “we”) is an education technology company based in Agona Swedru, Central Region, Ghana. We provide smart student ID cards and campus Wi-Fi to schools. This policy explains how we handle personal information in Adzesua HQ, the internal system our team uses to run the business, and in the SMS messages we send from it.

We follow Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Who can use Adzesua HQ

Adzesua HQ is for Adzesua Connect staff only. Nobody can create an account on their own: the managing director must invite each person by email first. People who sign in without an invitation see no business records; we keep only their name and email so the administrator can review the request, and we delete it if access is not granted.

Information we collect

  • When you sign in: your name, email address and, if you use Google sign-in, the basic profile Google shares (name, email and profile picture). We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Staff records: details our team enters to run the company, such as job title, phone number, leave and payroll information.
  • Customer records: details of partner schools and their contacts (names, roles, phone numbers, email addresses), invoices, payments and support requests.
  • Smart card records: details schools provide to produce student ID cards, such as student name, class, house and a guardian’s phone number.
  • SMS records: the phone numbers we text, the message, when it was sent and whether it was delivered.

How we use it

  • To let authorised staff sign in and see only the parts of the system their role allows.
  • To deliver our services to schools: producing cards, providing Wi-Fi, billing and support.
  • To send SMS messages such as payment receipts, invoice reminders, service notices and security alerts to staff.
  • To keep accounts, pay staff and meet our tax and legal obligations.
  • To protect the system, for example by alerting the administrator to unknown sign-in attempts.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that run the system for us, and only for that purpose:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) stores the data and handles sign-in. Data is stored on Google’s servers, which may be outside Ghana, under Google’s security and privacy terms.
  • Arkesel, a Ghanaian SMS provider, delivers our text messages. It receives the phone number and the message text.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How we protect it

  • Access is by invitation only, with a verified email address, and each person sees only what their role needs.
  • Database rules on the server enforce these limits; payroll and pay details are restricted to the managing director, Finance and HR.
  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Secret keys, such as the SMS key, are kept on the server and never in the website.

How long we keep it

We keep business and accounting records for as long as the law requires (generally at least six years for financial records). Other records are kept while they are needed for the purpose they were collected for and then deleted. Staff access is removed when someone leaves the company.

Text messages

We text schools, guardians and staff about services they use with us. Anyone who no longer wants non-essential messages can tell us using the contact details below and we will stop. We will still send messages you need for a service you have, such as a payment receipt.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact us using the details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
